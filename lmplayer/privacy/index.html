LM Player (com.sufu.lmplayer, "the App") is developed and maintained by Sufu lnc. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information on your Android device.

01

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, upload, store, or sell your personal information, video files, folder structures, watch history, subtitles, Private Vault PINs, biometric credentials, or browsing history on developer servers.

02

Local Media & Private Vault

Videos you play or import into the Private Vault remain strictly on your device. Private Vault files are encrypted locally on-device using Android Keystore-backed cryptography. You control all vault files and may export or permanently delete them at any time.

03

On-Device AI Subtitles & Translation

Subtitle translation and recognition run locally on your device. When you choose to download optional offline language models (such as Google ML Kit language packs), your device downloads those model files directly from Google's model distribution servers without sending your video or audio files to the developer.

04

Built-in Web Browser

When you browse third-party websites using the built-in web browser, your device connects directly to those websites. Those third-party websites operate under their own privacy policies. Bookmarks, browsing history, and cookies are stored locally on your device and can be cleared anytime from the Browser Menu.

05

Purchases & Subscriptions

LM Player Pro subscriptions and one-time purchases are processed securely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card details, billing address, or Google account password.

06

Device Permissions

  • Media / Video Storage: Used solely to discover and play local videos on your device and to save exported subtitles or downloaded videos.
  • Network / Internet: Used for streaming user-entered URLs, casting to local devices, downloading optional offline translation models, and Google Play Billing.
  • Biometrics: Optional unlock for your local Private Vault handled directly by Android's BiometricPrompt; the App never reads or stores biometric data.
  • Notifications: Used to display foreground download progress when you download a video.

07

Ads, Analytics & Tracking

LM Player contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, and no behavioral profiling.

08

Your Choices & Data Deletion

Because all app preferences, watch history, browser data, and Private Vault files are stored locally on your device, you can clear browser history from Browser Settings, delete vault items directly, or remove all app data by uninstalling LM Player or clearing app storage in Android Settings.

09

Contact Us
