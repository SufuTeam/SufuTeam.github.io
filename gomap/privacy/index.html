GoMap (com.sufu.trails, "the App") is developed and maintained by Sufu lnc. This Privacy Policy explains how GoMap handles location, activity, health, photo, and account information when you use the App on Android.

01

Overview & Guest Use

You can use GoMap immediately without creating an account. In guest mode, your saved routes, planned tracks, GPX/FIT imports, recorded activities, personal notes, and city achievements are stored locally on your device.

02

Location & Activity Recording

GoMap requests foreground precise/approximate location only to show your position on outdoor maps, discover nearby routes and peaks, compute real-time speed/grade on the cycling dashboard, and record GPS tracks when you explicitly start an activity. During an active recording, GoMap runs an Android foreground service with a persistent notification so tracking continues while the screen is off until you pause or finish.

Recorded tracks stay private on your device unless you explicitly enable optional account sync, share a private link, publish to the community, or opt in to Nearby Hikers during an active hike. You can obscure start and finish areas before sharing any route or activity publicly.

03

Health Connect Data

If you explicitly grant permission through Android Health Connect, GoMap reads only the workout data types you approve (exercise sessions, distance, steps, heart rate, active calories burned, elevation gained, and historical workout range) so you can view your outdoor activity history and metrics inside GoMap.

GoMap requests read-only access and never writes or modifies Health Connect records. Imported health records are stored strictly for your personal viewing and are never used for advertising, sold to third parties, shared with data brokers, or published automatically to the community. You can revoke Health Connect permissions at any time in Android Settings or the Health Connect app.

The use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

04

Optional Account, Cloud Sync & Connectors

If you choose to sign in using a GoMap password account, Google Sign-In, or Sign in with Apple, we store your account identifier, display name, optional profile photo, and authentication credentials on api.sufuapp.com over HTTPS so your private routes, lists, journeys, and activities sync across your devices.

If you explicitly connect an optional third-party fitness platform (such as Strava, Garmin, or Huawei Health) via OAuth2, GoMap uses the granted token solely to import your chosen workouts and tracks. You can disconnect any platform at any time.

05

Map, Terrain & Weather Services

To render online outdoor maps, calculate road/trail routing, look up elevation tiles, and fetch trailhead weather forecasts, GoMap requests tiles and data from Mapbox, OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap), AWS Open Data Terrain Tiles, NOAA GFS, and Open-Meteo. These requests transmit map coordinates, tile bounds, and your IP address directly to the respective service providers and never include your GoMap account credentials, private journal entries, personal photos, or heart rate data.

06

Device Permissions

  • Location (Foreground & Foreground Service): Used for map positioning, nearby trail search, navigation, cycling dashboard speed, and user-initiated GPS track recording.
  • Physical Activity Recognition: Used to improve auto-pause and motion accuracy during outdoor workouts.
  • Notifications: Used for the active recording foreground notification and optional off-route alerts.
  • Health Connect (Optional, Read-Only): Used only when enabled by you to import workout summaries, distance, steps, heart rate, elevation, and active calories.
  • Notification Listener (Optional, Read-Only): Used only if explicitly enabled in Android Settings to show the currently playing track title on the cycling dashboard without controlling playback or uploading data.
  • Photos (System Photo Picker): When you attach photos to an activity or profile, GoMap uses Android's system Photo Picker and never requests broad photo library access.

07

Ads, Analytics & Tracking

GoMap contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no cross-app behavioral tracking, and does not sell or rent user data.

08

Your Choices & Account Deletion

You can export a full local backup, delete individual routes or activities, disconnect Health Connect or cloud platforms, sign out on your device, or clear all local app data by uninstalling GoMap or clearing storage in Android Settings.

To request permanent deletion of a GoMap cloud account and all associated server-side profile, synced library, and community data, email littlejun56@gmail.com from your registered email or include your GoMap username. Verified deletion requests are completed within 7 days.
